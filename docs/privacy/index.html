CakeSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

CakeSync is an Android app that helps home bakers find, organise and share their cake photos and keep track of orders. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: CakeSync has no account and no server of its own. Your photos, orders, menu and settings stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it somewhere, such as your own Google Drive or a share to Instagram or WhatsApp.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional and off by default. If you connect it, CakeSync asks for the drive.file permission, which only lets the app see and manage files and folders that CakeSync itself creates. It cannot read the rest of your Drive. Uploaded files are stored in your Drive under Google's terms. You can disconnect in the app's Settings, or remove CakeSync's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

CakeSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the photo, caption or order form you chose is handed to the app you pick (for example Instagram, WhatsApp, email or text messages). What happens to it after that is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

The order form page that CakeSync links to does not load or send anything by itself. What your customer types is sent only when they tap Send, directly to you through the channel they pick.

Data the developer collects

None. CakeSync has no analytics, no advertising and no developer-run server. The developer cannot see your photos, orders, customers or Drive files.

Third-party components

CakeSync uses Google's on-device ML Kit and Google Play services to analyse photos and to sign in to Google. These components run on your phone, but Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about them (such as device and app information and performance metrics) under Google's Privacy Policy. One optional feature, background removal, downloads its model through Google Play services the first time you use it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything CakeSync stores lives in the app's storage on your phone and in the folders it creates in your gallery, calendar and (if connected) Google Drive. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the app's own storage. Photos saved to your gallery, calendar events and Drive files remain yours and can be deleted from those apps.

Children

CakeSync is a business tool for bakers and is not directed at children under 13.
